A small toolkit for reading text the way a computer reads it: which words carry feeling, which words repeat, which language it is written in, and how long it is. Everything runs on this page. There is no server, no sign up, and nothing is uploaded.

Honest note: the sentiment and keyword results come from simple word lists and counting rules, not from a trained model. They are a transparent starting point, not a verdict.

    How it works

    Sentiment

    Each word is checked against a short list of positive and negative words in English, Arabic, Bengali, Hindi and Urdu. Matched words are shown so you can see exactly why a score came out the way it did.

    Keywords

    Words are counted, common filler words are dropped, and the ten most frequent remain. No ranking magic, just counting.

    Language

    Characters are grouped by script: Latin, Arabic script, Bengali script and Devanagari. Urdu is separated from Arabic by letters that only Urdu uses. Mixed text shows the share of each script.

    Limits

    Word lists are small, so short or unusual text can score neutral even when the feeling is clear to a human. That is the honest limit of a rule based tool, and it is why the matched words are shown.
